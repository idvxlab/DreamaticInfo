---
name: composition
description: Plan whole-page hierarchy, spatial relations, safe regions and responsive reflow.
---
Start with communication and content regions, then art direction/composition, then components. Define grid, hero, text/chart/source areas, whitespace and alignment/anchoring/layering/cropping. Protect text, numbers, legends and sources with safe regions. Responsive media use flow/reflow; posters may use coordinates. Mark intentional overlaps with data-overlap=allow, but never obscure necessary information.

Choose an editorial focal point before boxes. State core_message, information_hierarchy and primary_visual; connect area, scale, contrast and placement to importance. Keep lower-priority evidence accessible without equal visual weight. Compose a subject-specific whole with varied density and breathing space. Typography or a chart may be the main visual; imagery is not mandatory. Explain why repeated containers are suitable where used. Define foreground/background/decorative layers, anchors, crops and safe overlaps. Decorative paths guide attention; factual connections require actual evidence. Do not copy a reference layout independent of content.
