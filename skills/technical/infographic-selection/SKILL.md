description: Choose content task, reading mode and delivery medium independently.
---
Distinguish statistical overview, comparison/ranking, temporal evolution, process/decision, hierarchy, geography, relationship flow, explanation and guide. Justify the main task; combinations are allowed. Reading mode and medium are independent. Deliver one selected design by default. Do not invent a dramatic story for explanatory material. Maps, networks and Sankey diagrams lack contracts; do not bypass them with illustration.
