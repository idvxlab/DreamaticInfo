Interactions must support keyboards. Mobile reflow preserves explanation and sources while optional decoration may be hidden. Print expands necessary interactive content and disables animation; do not depend on hover or animation completion. HTML is self-contained without CDNs, remote fonts or runtime resources. PDF is on demand. Preserve data-component-id/data-fact-id/data-asset-id traceability.
