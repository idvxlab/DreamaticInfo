---
name: quality-review
description: Inspect program diagnostics separately from Designer visual judgement.
---
Check data/references, then isolated desktop/mobile/print diagnostics, then Designer screenshot review. Name the issue, component and owner: builder for implementation, designer for composition/assets, analyst for facts, researcher for source material. Default to one review and local repairs, with at most two repair rounds. Program checks do not prove aesthetic quality; visual models do not replace source recomputation.

Before formal design submission, self-review core message, editorial weights, subject-specific focal concept, typography, ground, decorative purpose and spatial rhythm. Cite concrete regions and expected outcomes; revise generic choices. Structural validation is not visual approval.

After rendering, judge both design adequacy and implementation fidelity. An accurately implemented but weak composition is a Designer issue. Missing/misplaced planned treatments are Builder issues. Give location, observation, reading impact and bounded repair target. Do not demand arbitrary decoration, image counts or fewer cards. Do not reject a coherent typography-only composition. Check fallback and actual font effects, not merely asset presence. Never certify aesthetics from geometry checks alone.
